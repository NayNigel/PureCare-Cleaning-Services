Frequently asked questions

Which areas does PureCare serve?

PureCare currently lists Sandton, Midrand and Rosebank in Johannesburg as its service areas.

How do I request a cleaning quote?

Complete the service enquiry form with your property type, preferred date and cleaning requirements. PureCare will review the request and confirm the quote.

Is my preferred appointment date confirmed immediately?

No. The date on the enquiry form is a preferred date only. The PureCare team must confirm availability.
